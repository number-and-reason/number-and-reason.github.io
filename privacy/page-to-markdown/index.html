Privacy Policy — Page to Markdown for AI

Last updated: 2026-09-26 · Publisher: Number & Reason (Hangzhou) Digital Technology Co Ltd (数与理（杭州）数字科技有限公司)

Summary: this extension does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data or web page content.

  • What it reads. Only when you click the extension's icon or its right-click menu item, it reads the content of the current tab (or the text you selected) in order to convert it into Markdown.
  • Where processing happens. Entirely inside your browser, on your device. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code.
  • What it stores. Your settings (default mode, optional text to add at the top, image/link options, context size) are saved in your browser's local extension storage. A short-lived note of a right-click request is kept in session storage and discarded within two minutes. You can remove all of it by uninstalling the extension.
  • Clipboard and downloads. Text is copied to your clipboard or saved as a .md file only when you press the corresponding button.
  • Third parties. No data is transferred to us or to any third party. Links you choose to open (for example "More tools") are handled by your browser like any other link.
  • Children. The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
  • Contact. jileilei@shuyuli.cn

隐私政策（中文）

本扩展不收集、不在任何服务器上保存、不出售、不共享任何个人数据或网页内容。只在你点击图标或右键菜单时读取当前标签页（或选中文字）并在你的浏览器本地转换；扩展自身不发起任何网络请求，不含统计、追踪或广告代码。设置保存在浏览器本地的扩展存储里，右键请求的临时记录保存在会话存储中、两分钟内作废，卸载扩展即全部删除。联系：jileilei@shuyuli.cn
