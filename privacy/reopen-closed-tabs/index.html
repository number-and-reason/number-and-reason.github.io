Privacy Policy — Reopen Closed Tabs

Last updated: 2026-09-26 · Publisher: Number & Reason (Hangzhou) Digital Technology Co Ltd (数与理（杭州）数字科技有限公司)

Summary: this extension keeps your closed-tab history and saved sessions only on your own device. It does not send, sell or share any data.

  • What it reads. The title and address of your open tabs, so that it can list a tab after you close it and save the tabs of a window as a session. Tabs in Incognito windows are never read or recorded. Browser pages (such as chrome:// pages and the new tab page) are not recorded.
  • Where it is stored. In your browser's local extension storage on this device: the list of closed tabs (kept for the number of days and items you choose, 7 days and 500 items by default), your saved sessions and your settings. A temporary list of currently open tabs is kept in session storage and discarded when the browser closes.
  • No network. The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code. Site icons are read from Chrome's local icon cache.
  • Export and import. Sessions are written to a file only when you press Export, and read from a file only when you choose one to import.
  • Your control. You can clear the closed-tab history in Settings, delete sessions at any time, and remove everything by uninstalling the extension.
  • Third parties. No data is transferred to us or to any third party. Links you choose to open (for example "More tools") are handled by your browser like any other link.
  • Children. The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
  • Contact. hello@numberandreason.com

隐私政策（中文）

本扩展只在你自己的设备上保存关闭记录和会话，不发送、不出售、不共享任何数据。为了在你关闭标签页后列出它、以及把窗口保存为会话，扩展会读取已打开标签页的标题和网址；无痕窗口的标签页从不读取或记录，浏览器内部页面不记录。关闭记录（按你设置的天数和条数保留，默认 7 天、500 条）、会话和设置保存在浏览器本地的扩展存储里；当前打开标签页的临时列表保存在会话存储中，关闭浏览器即清除。扩展自身不发起任何网络请求，不含统计、追踪或广告代码；网站图标读取自 Chrome 本地缓存。只有你点“导出”才写文件、选择文件时才导入。可在设置里清空记录、随时删除会话，卸载扩展即全部删除。联系：hello@numberandreason.com
