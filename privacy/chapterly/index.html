Privacy Policy — Chapterly

Last updated: 2026-09-26 · Publisher: Number & Reason (Hangzhou) Digital Technology Co Ltd (数与理（杭州）数字科技有限公司)

Summary: Chapterly does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any page content or personal data.

  • What it reads. Only when you click the toolbar icon or press the shortcut, it reads the content of the current tab to show it in the reading view. The content is processed in that tab only and is discarded when you close the view.
  • What it stores. Your typography preferences (font, size, line height, width, theme) in your browser's local extension storage.
  • No network. The extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising code. Images in the article load from the website as they would on the page itself.
  • Third-party code. It includes Mozilla Readability (Apache License 2.0), bundled and unmodified.
  • Your control. Uninstalling removes the stored preferences.
  • Children. Not directed at children; collects no data from anyone.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
  • Contact. jileilei@shuyuli.cn
