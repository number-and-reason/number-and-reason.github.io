Privacy Policy — Creator Tally

Last updated: 2026-09-26 · Publisher: Number & Reason (Hangzhou) Digital Technology Co Ltd (数与理（杭州）数字科技有限公司)

Summary: Creator Tally does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

  • What it uses. Only the numbers and notes you type in yourself. The extension does not read web pages, does not access social media accounts or platform data, and makes no network requests.
  • What it stores. Your settings and the checks you choose to save (the name or link you enter, the numbers, the results and your note), in your browser's local extension storage on this device. Saved checks are deleted automatically after the period you choose (180 days by default) and are limited to 500.
  • Export and import. A file is written only when you press Export, and read only when you choose a file to import.
  • Your control. Delete single records or all records at any time; uninstalling removes everything from this device.
  • No tracking. No analytics, tracking or advertising code.
  • Children. The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
  • Contact. jileilei@shuyuli.cn

隐私政策（中文）

达人计数簿不收集、不传输、不出售、不共享任何数据。扩展只使用你自己输入的数字和备注，不读取网页、不访问社交平台账号或数据、不发起任何网络请求。设置和你主动保存的核验记录（你填写的名称或链接、数字、结果和备注）只保存在本机浏览器的扩展存储中，按你选择的期限自动删除（默认 180 天），最多 500 条。只有你点“导出”才写文件、选择文件时才导入。可随时单删或清空，卸载即全部删除。无统计、追踪或广告代码。联系：jileilei@shuyuli.cn
